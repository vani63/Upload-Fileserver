FAQ

What’s the story behind my travel blog?

It all started on a rainy afternoon in a dusty Parisian bookstore, where I stumbled upon a handwritten journal detailing a stranger's forgotten adventures. Inspired by their fearless spirit, I impulsively bought a one-way ticket to Bangkok the very next morning, trading my predictable corporate routine for a backpack. I initially launched this blog just to keep my family from worrying, documenting my chaotic train rides, cultural mishaps, and newfound love for street food. However, what began as a simple digital diary quickly blossomed into a vibrant community of fellow wanderers, eager to explore the world's hidden corners right alongside me.

How do I plan, document, and share my travels?

The biggest mistake new travelers make is over-scheduling. A rigid itinerary leaves no room for the magic of spontaneity.
* Book the Anchors: Secure your flights, your first two nights of accommodation, and any "must-do" experiences that sell out months in advance (like hiking the Inca Trail). Leave the rest open.
* The Rule of Halves: Plan half of your day and leave the other half completely blank. This allows you to wander down an interesting alleyway or accept a dinner invitation from a local without ruining your schedule.
